## 🔑 Key Terminology

- **SRM (Solar Radiation Management):** Techniques that reflect sunlight to reduce warming (e.g., SAI, MCB)
- **CDR (Carbon Dioxide Removal):** Techniques that remove CO₂ from the atmosphere (separate from SRM)
- **Stratospheric Aerosol Injection (SAI):** The most-discussed SRM method — mimicking volcanic cooling by injecting sulfate aerosols into the stratosphere
- **GeoMIP:** Geoengineering Model Intercomparison Project — coordinated SRM simulation experiments
- **SRM Governance Gap:** The absence of international agreements regulating SRM research and deployment
